# Company FAQ & Policies

## Refund Policy
Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days of approval. Customers must request a refund within 30 days of purchase.

## Shipping
We offer standard (5-7 days) and express (1-2 days) shipping. Free shipping is available for orders over $50.

## Contact Support
Our support team is available 24/7 via chat. For phone support, call 1-800-AI-SUPPORT between 9 AM and 5 PM EST.

## Account Security
We use multi-factor authentication (MFA) to secure all user accounts. Please do not share your password with anyone.
